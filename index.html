What sets us apart

We mock up the steel before we build it

Before manufacturing starts, we lay out the steel section locations against your floor plan. Every penetration you'll need — shower waste, toilet flange, sink and greywater lines — gets clear space to pass through the floor.

It means you won't find a chassis rail sitting exactly where your shower drain has to go, and you won't be cutting, re-routing or compromising your layout on site to work around steel that's already welded. A bit of extra time up front, and a very expensive problem avoided later.

Configuration

Two Height Options for Your Tiny Home Trailer

Choose your underfloor height based on your toilet system and design requirements.

Standard Height

Suitable for the most common toilet systems.

  • Flushing toilets
  • Incinerating toilets
  • Self-contained composting toilets
  • Through-floor composting toilets with underfloor storage tanks (in certain circumstances)

Raised Height

500mm underfloor clearance for through-floor systems.

  • Clivus Multrum LP (requires 500mm clearance)
  • GreenLoo GL90 (requires 500mm clearance)
  • Through-floor composting toilets with underfloor storage tank
