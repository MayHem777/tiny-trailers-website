Our Story

The Foundation Your Tiny Home Deserves

At Tiny Trailers, we build the trailers that tiny homes are built on. Based in Noosaville on the Sunshine Coast, we specialise in engineer-designed, Rover-approved tiny home trailers constructed from Duragal steel.

Every trailer we build complies with all relevant Australian Design Rules (ADRs) and comes with a VIN plate — giving you the peace of mind that your home has a legitimate, registerable foundation.

We work closely with tiny home builders across Queensland, and our trailers are the foundation for many of the homes built at Sunshine Tiny Houses.

Rover Approved ADR Compliant Engineer Designed VIN Registered Duragal Construction

Why Duragal

Steel That Lasts

Duragal is a hot-dip galvanised steel specifically designed for structural applications. It resists corrosion far better than standard mild steel — critical when your trailer will be parked outdoors, potentially in coastal environments, for decades.

Combined with electric brakes on all axles, heavy-duty jack stands, and low-profile tyres rated to 900kg per wheel, our trailers are engineered to support the full weight of a tiny home safely and reliably.
